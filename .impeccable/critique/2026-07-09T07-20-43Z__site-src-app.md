## Overall Impression

LingTour already has a point of view. The work in this pass was about removing the places where the site briefly fell back into generic web-app habits: hardcoded shared-nav language, compressed mobile card layouts, and a few shop/list surfaces that drifted away from the journal system.

## What's Working

- The field-journal brand language is real: Georgia display type, deep teal structure, restrained cinnabar/gold accents, and image framing create a memorable surface.
- Major public pages share a clear editorial rhythm instead of default dashboard scaffolding.
- The checkout and interpreting flows already have a believable operational tone rather than fake lifestyle copy.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Shared chrome locale drift**
  - **Why it matters**: Hardcoded nav labels and English-only route discovery break trust immediately for Chinese readers and make the product feel unfinished.
  - **Fix**: Route all shared navigation and routes-discovery copy through locale-aware translation keys; fetch locale-specific route data in the mega menu.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P1] Mobile browse cards were too compressed**
  - **Why it matters**: Routes and culture cards forced image and text into cramped side-by-side layouts on small screens, hurting scanning and story comprehension.
  - **Fix**: Return those cards to stacked mobile layouts, then preserve rotation and collage energy on larger breakpoints only.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

- **[P1] Shop collections drifted toward template card styling**
  - **Why it matters**: Rounded dark-image cards felt more like a generic e-commerce template than a field-journal archive.
  - **Fix**: Rebuild the collection entries as framed specimens with visible editorial copy and straight-edge journal surfaces.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Home hero voice was split across mismatched copy systems**
  - **Why it matters**: A small translated eyebrow, hardcoded English display title, and unrelated CTA labels made the first fold feel half-finished.
  - **Fix**: Consolidate the hero into one translation-backed copy system with clearer CTA language.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Residual hierarchy density remains in checkout and interpreting**
  - **Why it matters**: These pages are functional, but still ask users to parse too many equal-weight elements in a few places.
  - **Fix**: Tighten section priority, reassurance copy, and recovery language in a future pass.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`
